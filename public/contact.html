Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about accounts, deliveries, and data isolation.

How does user data isolation work in M.K. Store?
Each registered customer receives a cryptographically secure, unique UUID (Dataspace ID). Your cart items, saved delivery addresses, and order history are isolated specifically to your account and never mixed with any other customer's data.
Can I explore the store and view products without logging in?
Yes! Anyone can freely browse our entire catalog, view product details, check prices, and inspect categories without logging in. Sign in is only requested when you are ready to add items to your private cart or place an order.
What are your delivery timelines and shipping charges?
Orders over $50 qualify for 100% Free Shipping! For smaller orders, a standard $5 delivery fee applies. Most orders are dispatched within 24 hours with real-time tracking links provided.
